If You Need Help Right Now

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, call 911 now.

This page lists crisis and emergency resources available 24/7. Manhattan Mental Health Counseling is not a crisis service. We cannot provide immediate emergency support through our website, contact form, office phone line, voicemail, email, client portal, or therapist matching process.

Immediate Danger

Call 911

Use this if someone is in immediate danger, may seriously harm themselves or someone else, needs urgent medical help, or needs police, fire, or EMS response.

📞 Call 911

24/7 Crisis Resources

Free, confidential support available anytime — day or night.

24/7 · Free · Confidential

Suicide, Self-Harm, Emotional Crisis, or Substance-Use Crisis

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

You do not have to be suicidal to contact 988. Use it for suicidal thoughts, emotional distress, substance use concerns, relationship distress, loneliness, depression, anxiety, or concern about someone else.

📞 Call 988 💬 Text 988 🖥️ Chat with 988
24/7 · Free · Confidential

Text-Based Crisis Support

Crisis Text Line

Available 24/7, free and confidential in the United States. This can be helpful if talking by phone feels too difficult or unsafe.

💬 Text HOME to 741741
NYC · 24/7 · 200+ Languages

NYC-Based Mental Health Crisis Support

NYC 988 / NYC Well

Free, confidential mental health and substance use support, 24/7/365, in more than 200 languages.

📞 Call 988 📞 Call NYC Well 💬 Text WELL to 65173
NYC Only

NYC Mobile Crisis Teams

If someone in NYC is experiencing a serious behavioral health crisis but does not appear to need immediate hospitalization, you can request a Mobile Crisis Team by calling 988. They may provide assessment, crisis intervention, supportive counseling, follow-up support, and referrals.

If there is immediate danger or urgent medical risk, call 911 instead.

📞 Call 988

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Confidential support and resources for domestic violence victims. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Safety note: If your device, browser, or internet activity may be monitored, use a safer device if possible and consider clearing your browsing history.

Child Safety

Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911. For reporting or support, the Childhelp hotline is available 24/7/365 by call, text, or chat.

📞 Call 1-800-422-4453

Report Suspected Child Abuse in NYC / New York State

General public: Call 311 in NYC, or call the NY State Central Register at 1-800-342-3720.
Mandated reporters: Call 311 in NYC, or call 1-800-635-1522.

📞 Call 311 📞 NY State Central Register

Substance Use or Treatment Referral

SAMHSA National Helpline

Free, confidential treatment referrals, information, and connection to local treatment facilities, support groups, and community-based organizations. Available 24/7/365 in English and Spanish.

Not in Crisis? Start Therapy

If you are not in immediate danger and would like to start therapy, Manhattan Mental Health Counseling provides online therapy across New York State. Our team responds during business hours.

Do not use our contact form, voicemail, email, client portal, or scheduling process for emergencies or any situation where someone may be in immediate danger.

Non-Crisis Resources

The resources below are not for emergencies and may not be available 24/7. If someone may be in immediate danger, call 911. If someone needs urgent emotional support, call or text 988.

Therapy & Referral Options

Outpatient therapy, specialty referral directories, and professional treatment matching services.

Support Groups & Community-Based Care

LGBTQ+ peer and family support, addiction recovery groups, youth and family resources, and other community-based supports.

Legal, Financial & Housing Assistance

Housing legal help, emergency rent or utility assistance, social-service locators, nutrition support, and other practical resources.

Self-Care Apps

Self-care, mindfulness, mood tracking, habit-building, and meditation apps — non-crisis tools only.

Education Materials

Worksheets, psychoeducation, DBT resources, anxiety coping materials, and domestic violence education — non-crisis educational resources.

Manhattan Mental Health Counseling provides online therapy across New York State. We are not a crisis hotline, emergency service, hospital, mobile crisis team, or 24/7 response service.
